# Learning Capability Gaps

## Core rule

Prioritize new capability work from observed demand and execution evidence, not novelty.

## Workflow

- Aggregate missing or blocking capability IDs across qualified opportunities and executions.
- Preserve counts, economic context, failure reasons, and verification gaps separately.
- Distinguish a missing capability from missing authority or missing credentials.
- Rank candidates by recurring demand, attainable value, implementation cost, verification feasibility, and risk.
- Recommend `build-skill`, `add-tool`, `add-plugin`, `improve-verifier`, `retain-human-gate`, or `do-not-build`.

## Output

Return ranked capability gaps with supporting observations and the smallest justified next investment. Do not claim projected revenue as realized revenue.
